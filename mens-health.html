Understanding the Male Reproductive System

Key Organs

  • •

    Penis

    External reproductive organ for urination and sexual function.

  • •

    Testes (Testicles)

    Produce sperm and male hormone testosterone.

  • •

    Prostate

    Produces fluid that protects and nourishes sperm.

  • •

    Scrotum

    Protective sac holding the testes.

Why They Matter: These organs control reproduction, hormone production, and sexual health. Understanding them helps you maintain health and catch problems early.

Sexual & Reproductive Health

Importance of Hygiene

  • ✓ Wash genitals daily with warm water
  • ✓ Retract foreskin (if uncircumcised) and clean gently
  • ✓ Keep area dry to prevent fungal infections
  • ✓ Wash hands before and after sexual contact
  • ✓ Use clean towels

Normal Body Changes

  • Puberty (12-18 years): Voice deepens, body hair grows, genitals enlarge. This is normal development.
  • Erection: Normal response to sexual stimulation or thoughts. Frequency varies by age.
  • Testicular Size: One testicle is often slightly larger. This is normal.
  • Penis Variation: Size and appearance vary naturally. Understand and accept your body.

Common Diseases & Problems

UTI (Urinary Tract Infection)

What: Infection in the urinary system

Causes: Bacteria, poor hygiene, unprotected intercourse

Symptoms: Burning urination, frequent urination, cloudy urine

When to See Doctor: Symptoms lasting >2 days

Prevention: Stay hydrated, urinate after intercourse, practice hygiene

Erectile Dysfunction

What: Difficulty achieving or maintaining erection

Causes: Stress, anxiety, health conditions, medications

Symptoms: Persistent difficulty with erections

When to See Doctor: If affecting your quality of life

Prevention: Manage stress, exercise, healthy diet, sleep well

Prostate Problems

What: Enlargement or infection of the prostate

Causes: Age, hormonal changes, infection

Symptoms: Difficulty urinating, weak stream, frequent urination

When to See Doctor: Any urinary symptoms

Prevention: Regular check-ups after 40, healthy lifestyle

STIs (Sexually Transmitted Infections)

What: Infections passed through sexual contact

Causes: Bacterial, viral, or parasitic agents

Symptoms: Discharge, pain, sores, itching

When to See Doctor: Any unusual symptoms

Prevention: Safe sex, regular testing, vaccinations

Testicular Infections

What: Inflammation of the testicles or epididymis

Causes: Bacterial or viral infections, injury

Symptoms: Swelling, pain, warmth, fever

When to See Doctor: Immediately with pain or swelling

Prevention: Practice safe sex, protect genitals during sports

Life Stages Guidance

🧑 Adolescence (10-19 years)

Your body is changing rapidly. Hormones increase, genitals develop, and you may experience nocturnal emissions. This is completely normal. Understand your body and practice hygiene.

💼 Adulthood (20-40 years)

Your peak fertility and sexual function years. Regular health check-ups, safe practices, and maintaining fitness are important for long-term health and sexual wellness.

👨‍👧 Fatherhood & Partnership

Whether planning parenthood or in relationships, understanding fertility, sexual health, and responsible practices are essential. Support your partner's reproductive health as well.

🧓 Aging (50+ years)

Hormone levels change, prostate issues may arise, and sexual function may shift. Regular check-ups, healthy lifestyle, and open communication are vital during this phase.

Myths vs Facts

❌ Myth:

Men don't get UTIs or reproductive infections.

✅ Fact:

Men can get UTIs and STIs. They may have fewer symptoms but need the same treatment. Practice safe sex and maintain hygiene.

❌ Myth:

Talking about sexual problems with a doctor is shameful.

✅ Fact:

Doctors are trained professionals. Open communication helps diagnosis and treatment. Your health matters more than embarrassment.
