# 🩺 SwasthyaSetu – Reproductive & Sexual Health Awareness Platform

🔗 **Live Website:**  
http://localhost:8000/

SwasthyaSetu is an awareness-based educational web platform designed to provide **stigma-free, culturally sensitive, and medically accurate** information on reproductive and sexual health for both **men and women**. The platform bridges the information gap by offering structured learning, visual clarity, and trusted resources in a user-friendly format.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Create a **safe and educational environment** for reproductive health learning  
- Provide **separate, tailored sections** for Men and Women  
- Break myths and taboos through **simple, clear explanations**  
- Combine **education + e-commerce** for health resources  
- Ensure **responsive, accessible, and modern UI**

---

## 🧩 Key Features

### 👩 Women’s Health
- Female anatomy  
- Menstrual cycle  
- PCOS, Endometriosis, Infections  
- Nutrition & lifestyle guidance  
- Modules:
  - Sexual health & contraception  
  - Pregnancy & fertility  
  - Hormones & cycle management  
  - Menopause & life stages  
  - Breast & pelvic health  

### 👨 Men’s Health
- Male anatomy  
- Puberty & development  
- ED, prostate issues, STIs  
- Fitness & mental health  
- Modules:
  - Sexual function & fertility  
  - Testosterone & hormones  
  - Prostate & testicular health  
  - STI prevention  
  - Mental & emotional wellness  

### 🛒 Integrated Health Store
- Doctor-approved products  
- Gender-specific wellness items  
- Clean UI with call-to-action buttons  
- Education + commerce integration
